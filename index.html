🍽️ What's served
Breakfast

Hot options like eggs, breakfast meats, and potatoes, plus waffles, pancakes, and breakfast sandwiches. Yogurt, cereal, and fresh fruit always available.

Rotating pizzas, burgers, wraps, and quesadillas, plus a build-your-own salad bar and a fry station.

Dinner

Chef-prepared entrées that change through the week, alongside the salad bar, fry station, and grab-and-go options. Opening Day debuts a new Texas-style BBQ spread.
